Wedding invitation cards

Type a guest's name and how many people the card admits, then save or send the card.

Works on a phone or a computer. Saved cards go to your Downloads. On a phone, the Send button opens your share menu so you can pick WhatsApp.

Edit card wording

Changes show on the card straight away. The fonts, colours and positions stay the same; long text shrinks to fit.

Changes apply to the cards you save now. They are not kept after the page is closed or refreshed.
